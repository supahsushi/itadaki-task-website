Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Itadaki Task handles information when you use the iOS app and this support website. Itadaki Task is designed as a local, personal task-tracking app with a sushi-themed progress system.

Information Stored By The App

Itadaki Task may store the information you enter or create in the app on your device, including:

  • your customer name or display name;
  • task titles, categories, due dates, repeat settings, reminder settings, and completion status;
  • daily sushi count, lifetime task completions, streak progress, and best daily completion count;
  • achievement progress, unlocked badges, and collection/customer progress.

In the current reviewed app source, this information is stored locally using iOS device storage. We do not operate a separate account system or task-sync server for this data.

Notifications And Reminders

If you create a task with a reminder, Itadaki Task may ask iOS for notification permission. Local notifications may include the task title so you can recognize the reminder. Notification permission is controlled by iOS, and you can change it in Settings > Notifications > Itadaki Task.

Apple Health

Itadaki Task may request permission to read certain Apple Health data types, such as step count, active energy burned, and workouts. Apple Health access is optional and controlled by iOS. You can review or revoke Health permissions in the Health app or iOS Settings.

We do not sell Apple Health data. The current reviewed app source requests read permission only and does not request permission to write Health data.

Purchases

If you purchase Premium, the transaction is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The app may use App Store purchase status to unlock Premium features or restore a previous purchase.

Support Emails

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, device details, screenshots, and description of the issue. We use support messages to respond to you and troubleshoot the app.

Website Data

This support website is a lightweight static site. It does not include an account system, contact form, advertising scripts, or analytics scripts in the current files. If the site is hosted on GitHub Pages or another hosting provider, that provider may process basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, and request logs according to its own policies.

Children's Privacy

Itadaki Task is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support contact, please email us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Your Choices

  • You can edit or delete tasks inside the app where those controls are available.
  • You can turn off notifications in iOS Settings.
  • You can revoke Health access in iOS Settings or the Health app.
  • You can remove locally stored app data by deleting the app from your device.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date above.
